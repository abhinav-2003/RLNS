💡 Mail landing in Junk / Spam folder?
  • Recipient 1-Click Fix: The recipient can right-click the email in Junk and click "Not Junk" or "Add sender to Safe Senders list". All future emails will land directly in their Main Inbox.
  • Corporate Domains (e.g. @nfl.co.in): Corporate enterprise gateways automatically flag external emails with PDF attachments as Junk until the recipient marks the sender as trusted.
  • Optimized Delivery: Lease Billing Application automatically builds plain-text alternatives, reply-to routing, and standard priority headers to maximize inbox delivery.
